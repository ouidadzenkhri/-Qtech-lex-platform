# QTech Lex Platform

> Bridging Law and Technology.

QTech Lex Platform is a LegalTech project designed to bridge the gap between legal processes and software engineering by transforming legal and procedural concepts into structured digital tools.

The project explores how modern web technologies can be applied to support the organization, monitoring, and digital management of legal procedures, creating a practical intersection between legal methodology, procedural requirements, and technological innovation.

## What the Project Does

QTech Lex Platform provides a structured digital framework for organizing and monitoring legal case-related activities and procedural information.

Its core functionalities include:

- Legal Case Tracking — Organizing case-related information and monitoring procedural progress.
- Workflow Management — Structuring legal procedures and case workflows into a clear and manageable digital format.
- Deadline Monitoring — Tracking important procedural deadlines and legal timelines.
- Procedural Organization — Centralizing relevant information to facilitate structured case management.
- Responsive User Interface — Providing a clean, accessible, and responsive interface designed for efficient interaction across different devices.

The platform is designed as a foundation that can be further extended with advanced legal rules, automated calculations, notifications, calendar integration, and other LegalTech capabilities.

## Why the Project Is Useful

Legal procedures involve interconnected rules, procedural stages, deadlines, and case-related information. When these elements are managed manually or across disconnected systems, organizing and monitoring them can become complex and inefficient.

QTech Lex Platform addresses this challenge by exploring a structured digital approach to legal workflow and procedural deadline management.

The project demonstrates how software engineering can help translate legal and procedural requirements into organized, accessible, and technology-driven workflows.

It is particularly relevant to the intersection of:

- Public Law and Legal Studies
- Software Engineering
- Digital Transformation
- Legal Workflow Management
- Procedural Deadline Monitoring
- LegalTech Innovation

The platform is not intended to replace legal professionals, legal interpretation, or professional judgment. Instead, it demonstrates how technology can serve as a supporting layer for the organization, monitoring, and digital management of legal processes.

## Bridging Law and Technology

The central concept behind QTech Lex Platform is the integration of legal knowledge with software engineering.

Legal systems are built upon rules, procedures, deadlines, and structured processes, while software provides the ability to organize, process, monitor, and present complex information through digital systems.

QTech Lex Platform explores the space between these two disciplines by translating legal and procedural concepts into structured digital workflows.

This approach aims to reduce the gap between:

Legal Knowledge → Procedural Logic → Digital Implementation

By connecting these layers, the project demonstrates how LegalTech can contribute to the digital transformation of legal processes while preserving the importance of legal methodology and professional judgment.

## How to Get Started

### Prerequisites

To explore the current version of the project, you need:

- A modern web browser
- A local copy of the repository

The current frontend implementation does not require a complex installation process or external dependencies.

### Running the Project

1. Clone or download the repository.
2. Open the project directory.
3. Locate the main HTML file.
4. Open it in a modern web browser.
5. Explore the available interfaces and functionalities.

For development or modification, familiarity with HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript is recommended.

## Project Vision

QTech Lex Platform is built around the principle that legal knowledge and software engineering can complement one another to create practical digital solutions for legal processes.

The long-term vision is to evolve the platform into a comprehensive LegalTech environment capable of supporting:

- Structured legal workflows
- Procedural timelines
- Legal rules and requirements
- Case-related information
- Automated deadline calculations
- Digital notifications and reminders
- Calendar-based procedural tracking

Through this approach, QTech Lex Platform aims to contribute to the development of accessible, structured, and technology-driven LegalTech solutions while strengthening the connection between law, technology, and digital innovation.
